FAQ

Questions we often hear

What does your government training include?

We design cybersecurity and technology programs around your department: awareness sessions, hands-on workshops and role-based training for officials and IT staff. Programs can be delivered on-site, online or in a hybrid format, with assessments and certificates.

Do you work with clients outside Dehradun?

Yes. Hackademic is based in Dehradun and serves businesses, institutions and government teams across India. Most projects run smoothly online, with on-site visits when needed.

What is included in a security audit?

We define the scope with you, run vulnerability assessments and testing, review compliance against standards such as ISO 27001, and hand over a prioritised report with a remediation plan. We can also re-test once fixes are in place.

Can you handle branding and the website together?

Absolutely. Combining identity, UI/UX and development under one team keeps your brand consistent and speeds up delivery.

How does your digital marketing approach SEO?

We start with keyword research and a technical audit, then work on on-page content, local SEO and link building, with monthly reports so you can see traffic and lead growth.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on scope. After a short discovery call we share a clear timeline, deliverables and cost, so you know exactly what to expect before we start.

Let's talk about your next project

Tell us what you need and our team will follow up to discuss scope, timing, and next steps.
